# Everlance

> Everlance is an automatic mileage and expense tracking platform for self-employed professionals, gig workers, contractors, and businesses. Its apps capture GPS-based mileage, sync bank and card transactions to categorize expenses, surface tax deductions, and generate IRS-compliant reports. For teams and enterprises, Everlance offers a gated Business API for uni-directional data transfer into the platform. Everlance was acquired by Motus in February 2025.

## APIs

- [Everlance Business API](https://help.everlance.com/hc/en-us/sections/38201888806157-Everlance-Business-API): Enterprise data API for creating/updating user profiles, applying user attributes, assigning team structure and roles, and managing favorite places. Base URL https://api.everlance.com. Bearer-token auth; access is provisioned by the Everlance team.
